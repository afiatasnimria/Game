Game Rules

  • Move your mouse to control the player.
  • Avoid touching the expanding purple rings.
  • Touching a ring costs 1 life.
  • You start with 3 lives.
  • Green orbs give bonus points.
  • Surviving increases your score over time.
  • Higher waves make rings spawn faster.
  • Chain dodges create combo bonuses.
  • Game ends when all lives are lost.
