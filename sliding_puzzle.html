Sliding Puzzle

Moves: 0

Game Rules

  • The puzzle contains 8 numbered tiles and 1 empty space.
  • Your goal is to arrange the numbers from 1 to 8.
  • Tiles can move only into the empty space.
  • You can move a tile if it is directly next to the empty space.
  • Try to solve the puzzle in the minimum number of moves.
  • Press the Shuffle button to start a new game.
  • When all tiles are in correct order, you win.
