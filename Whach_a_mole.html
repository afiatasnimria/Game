Whack-a-Mole

Score: 0 | Time: 30

Game Rules

  • Moles will appear randomly from holes.
  • Click on the mole before it disappears.
  • Each successful hit gives you 1 point.
  • You have 30 seconds to score as much as possible.
  • Moles change position every 0.7 seconds.
  • If you miss, no points are added.
  • Try to get the highest score before time runs out.
